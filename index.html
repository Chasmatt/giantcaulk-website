Why joints fail

Most of the restoration work we're called into isn't a product failure. It's a prep failure. If you recognise more than one of these on your building, the sealant is not the problem.

  1. New sealant applied over old sealant instead of full removal
  2. Inadequate surface prep, or a contaminated substrate
  3. Wrong sealant chemistry for the substrate
  4. No backer rod, or the joint filled too deep — three-sided adhesion
  5. Joint cut too narrow for the movement it has to absorb
  6. Wrong product for the UV or traffic exposure
